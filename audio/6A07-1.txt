With many compact strollers, when you fold the stroller, the handlebar rotates downward.

It's a small detail, but it's something the parent experiences every time they fold or unfold the stroller.
So, I started thinking, can we make the folding motion more natural, while allowing the parent to stay more upright?

Let me explain what you just saw.
Before folding, the only thing the parent needs to do is bring the canopy to its lowest position to achieve the most compact folded size.
Then, the parent simply rotates the handlebar upward.
As the handlebar rotates, the seat rotates together with it, while all four wheels remain on the ground.
So the parent never needs to lift the stroller.
As the handlebar reaches the upright position, the seat also reaches the release position.
At this point, the seat automatically releases and folds by itself.
Then, the parent simply pushes the handlebar downward to complete the rest of the folding process.
When fully folded, the stroller remains standing on all four wheels.
And unfolding is simply the reverse of the folding process.
Because the handlebar remains upright, the parent can simply pull it upward to unfold the stroller, without needing to bend over or lift the stroller.
The seat can also fold in the parent -facing position, so there is no need to change the seat direction before folding.
The goal is to create a simple, continuous folding experience that feels natural, comfortable,
and effortless, without bending over or lifting the stroller.
