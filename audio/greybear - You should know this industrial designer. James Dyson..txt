James Dyson is a British industrial designer, and he got fed up in 1978 when his vacuum lost suction as the bag got clogged.
To fix this, he borrowed cyclone separating, which he saw being used in a local sawmill and started prototyping.
After five years and 5 ,127 prototypes, he had a working bagless prototype, but manufacturers were rejecting it.
So he went independent and launched his first mass -produced vacuum, which was the DC -01 in 1993. His core idea is ruthless.
If the design gets worse with use, that means the design has failed.
