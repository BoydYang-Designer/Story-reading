BOXY OPENING Good morning everyone. Thank you for joining us today.
We're excited to share one of our latest travel stroller concepts, BOXY.
Like every project we develop, this concept began with a simple question.
Why does traveling with a stroller still require so much effort?
When parents travel, they already have many things to manage.
Luggage, boarding, transportation, and taking care of their children.
However, a stroller which is supposed to make life easier, often becomes one more thing to carry, store, and work around.
Even after folding, traditional strollers usually create irregular shapes that are difficult to fit into car trunks, hotel rooms, or airport spaces.
So we challenged ourselves with a different idea.
What if a stroller could fold into one of the simplest and most efficient shapes possible, a box?
That question became the starting point of BOXY.
Our goal wasn't simply to make a smaller stroller, but to create one that is easier to carry, easier to store, and easier to travel with.
Before we move into the design details, I'd like to show you the prototype.
One of the key features of BOXY is its simple and intuitive folding experience.
The folding process only requires two simple movements. First, fold down the canopy. Then, push the handlebar downward. That's it.
Within seconds, the stroller transforms into a clean, compact box.
Unlike traditional strollers that fold into irregular shapes, BOXY creates a predictable rectangular form that is easier to lift, carry, store, and organize throughout your journey.
To make BOXY more travel -friendly for air travel, we designed the folded size with IATA cabin baggage recommendations in mind.
However, cabin acceptance may still vary depending on the airline's carry -on policy.
Opening the stroller is just as intuitive.
Simply reverse the same two movements. Lift the handlebar. Then, raise the canopy.
Within seconds, BOXY is ready to use again.
The interaction is designed to feel natural, effortless, and easy for first -time users.
Now that you've seen how BOXY works, let's take a closer look at the thinking behind the design.
Every design decision was guided by three key ideas. Simpler folding. More efficient storage. A cleaner travel experience.
To wrap up, BOXY is more than just another compact stroller.
It's designed to make family travel simpler.
By folding into a clean, box -shaped form, BOXY is easier to carry, easier to store, and easier to travel with. Our goal is simple. Less time managing the stroller. More time enjoying the journey. Thank you for your time.
We'd love to hear your thoughts and answer any questions you may have.
