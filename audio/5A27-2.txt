The idea behind wonder started with a very simple observation.
Parents often stop their stroller for just a few seconds to answer a question, check their phone,
or adjust something for their child.
But even during these short moments, the stroller may not be fully stable.
The ground may look flat, but there can still be a small slope.
And if the brake has not been used, the stroller may start to roll.
That simple situation became the starting point for wonder.
Two key ideas from this observation, we focused on two everyday problems. First, safer stopping.
We developed a mechanical auto -brake system.
When the stroller stops, the brake engages automatically.
When the parent pushes forward, the brake releases automatically.
So the basic interaction is very simple. Stop. It brakes. Push. It moves. Second, easier folding.
We wanted to make stroller folding feel like one continuous movement instead of several separate steps.
This became our one continuous motion folding system.
These are the two main ideas behind wonder.

Because this is a mechanical system, there is one important point we need to consider.
You can feel a small amount of force when starting from a complete stop.
This is the force needed to overcome the brake.
The amount of force can be adjusted and it is directly related to how much slope the system is designed to resist.
In our current prototype, the system can hold a stroller carrying a 20 kg load on an 8 degree slope.
So we can adjust the system to find the right balance between anti -roll performance and pushing comfort. Three brake modes. Further development.
This also led us to another design question.
What happens if the auto -brake push feeling is too strong for some users?
To explore this, we developed the idea of a three -mode brake system. The first is full lock.
The wheels are fully locked for parking or situations where maximum stability is needed. The second is auto -brake. This is the everyday mode.
The stroller locks automatically when it stops and releases when the user pushes forward.
And the third is free roll.
The brake is completely disengaged so the stroller can move freely without the auto -brake engagement feeling.
So instead of using one setting for every situation, parents can choose the level of control they prefer. Full lock. Auto -brake. Free roll.
This is still part of our development direction, but it gives us another way to balance safety, comfort, and user preference. Video. Folding knife.
