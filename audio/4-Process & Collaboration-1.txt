Can you give an example of a project where you had to adjust your design for safety or manufacturability?
On one stroller project, I designed a unique feature where the stroller's basket could rotate from the lower position to the upper position after removing the seat.
The basket could then be zipped up and expanded into a larger shopping basket, allowing parents to upcycle the stroller after their child outgrew it.
However, during development, we realized that the rotating mechanism was too complex to manufacture and would increase production costs.
To address this, I worked with the team to simplify the design.
Instead of rotation, the basket could be removed and reattached in the upper position.
This adjustment kept the upcycling concept intact while making the product safer, more practical,
and much more cost -efficient to manufacture.
