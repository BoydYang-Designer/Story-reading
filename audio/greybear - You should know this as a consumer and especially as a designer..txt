It isn't about obsession, it's about misplacement.
We are taught to believe that value lies within the object itself, rather than in the labor systems and people that made them.

Design and branding are central to this illusion.
Smooth surfaces, seamless fonts, minimal packaging and aspirational imagery don't make products more attractive, they just redirect attention.
