One Continuous Motion Folding The second key feature is our one continuous motion folding system.

When the handlebar reaches the bottom, it automatically starts the folding mechanism.
From there, the handlebar rotates down, and the seat moves with it.
There is no separate seat release. No extra folding step.

As I continue pushing the handlebar down, the folding process finishes automatically.
