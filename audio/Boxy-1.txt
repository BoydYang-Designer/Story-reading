BOXY Travel Stroller Concept Presentation Opening Good morning, everyone.
Thank you for joining us today.
Today, I'd like to introduce BOXY, one of our latest travel stroller concepts,
a project that began with a very simple question.
Why does traveling with a stroller still feel more difficult than it should?
When parents travel, they already have many things to manage, luggage, boarding, transportation,
and taking care of their children.
However, a stroller, which is supposed to make life easier, often becomes another thing to carry, store, and work around.
Even when traditional travel strollers fold into a compact size, they usually create an irregular shape that is difficult to organize efficiently in a car trunk,
a hotel room, or an airport.
So we challenged ourselves with a different idea.
What if a stroller could fold into one of the world's simplest and most efficient forms? A BOXY.
That simple question became the starting point of BOXY.
Today, I'd like to show you how this concept makes traveling with a stroller easier, cleaner, and more intuitive.
Live Demonstration Before I explain the design in more detail, let me first show you the product.
One of the key features of BOXY is its simple and intuitive folding experience.
The folding process requires only two natural actions. First, fold down the canopy.
Then, simply push the handlebar downward. That's it.
In just a few seconds, the stroller transforms into a clean, compact BOX.
This box -shaped form isn't simply a visual design statement.
It is a functional solution for traveling families.
Instead of folding into an irregular shape with components extending in different directions,
BOXY creates a predictable rectangular form that is easier to lift, carry, stack, and store.
Another important advantage is that the folded dimensions have been designed around the ITA cabin baggage size guidelines.
This allows parents to bring the stroller on board on many flights instead of checking it in,
subject to each airline's cabin baggage policy.
That means less waiting at baggage claim, less concern about damage during handling,
and a smoother travel experience from departure to arrival.
Now let me show you how easy it is to unfold.
Opening BOXY is just as intuitive.
Simply reverse the same two actions. First, lift the handlebar. Then, raise the canopy.
Within seconds, the stroller is ready to use again.
The entire interaction is designed to feel natural, requiring minimal effort and almost no learning.
Our goal wasn't simply to create a stroller that folds quickly.
We wanted to design a stroller that travels with you, not separately from you. Transition to the presentation.
Now that you've seen how BOXY works, I'd like to take you behind the design and share the thinking that shaped this concept. Let's move to the presentation.
Slide 1 Design Vision Every successful product begins with understanding people.
For this project, we didn't start by asking how to make a stroller smaller.
Instead, we asked how we could make traveling with a stroller easier.
Our goal was to reduce not only the physical size of the product, but also the mental burden of traveling with it.
Because good design should simplify the entire experience, not just one interaction.
Slide 2 User Insights Through our research and observations, we found that many travel strollers focus primarily on becoming smaller after folding. However,
reducing size alone doesn't solve every problem.
Their folded shapes are often irregular, making them difficult to organize, difficult to store efficiently, and inconvenient to transport.
Parents are still left wondering where to put the stroller and how to move it through the airport.
This led us to shift our focus from folding smaller to folding smarter.
Slide 3 Design Solution The result is BOXY.
Instead of creating another compact stroller, we designed one that transforms into one of the most familiar and efficient forms, a BOX. A BOX is predictable. It stacks easily. It stores efficiently. It integrates naturally with luggage.
In other words, the shape itself becomes part of the solution.
Rather than simply reducing the size, we redefined the final form.
Slide 4 Key Features Here are some of the key features of BOXY.
A clean box -shaped folding structure for efficient storage.
A simple two -step folding and unfolding experience.
Folded dimensions designed around the IETA cabin baggage size guidelines.
Easy to carry, stack, and transport throughout the journey.
A clean, minimal design language that reflects both functionality and modern aesthetic.
Together, these features create a smoother and more enjoyable travel experience for parents.
