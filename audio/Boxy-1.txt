Thank you Carol. Next I'd like to introduce our latest travel stroller concept, BOXY.
This concept began with a simple question.
Why does traveling with a stroller still require so much effort?
When parents travel, they already have many things to manage, luggage, boarding, transportation,
and taking care of their children.
However, a stroller, which is supposed to make life easier, often becomes one more thing to carry, store, and work around.
Even after folding, traditional strollers often remain bulky and awkward, making them difficult to store in car trunks, hotel rooms, or other tight spaces.
So we challenged ourselves with a different idea.
What if a stroller could fold into one of the simplest and most practical forms, a box?
That question became the starting point of BOXY.
Our goal wasn't simply to make a smaller stroller, but to create one that is easier to carry, easier to store, and easier to travel with.
Before we get into the design details, let me show you how BOXY works.
One of the key features of BOXY is its simple and intuitive folding experience.
The folding process only requires two simple movements. First, fold down the canopy. Then, push the handlebar downward. That's it.
Within seconds, the stroller transforms into a clean, compact box.
Once folded, BOXY creates a clean, predictable rectangular form that is easier to lift, carry, and store throughout your journey.
To make BOXY more suitable for air travel, we designed the folded size with IATA cabin baggage size guidelines in mind.
However, cabin acceptance may still vary depending on the airline's carry -on policy.
Opening the stroller is just as intuitive.
Simply reverse the same two movements. Lift the handlebar. Then, raise the canopy.
Within seconds, BOXY is ready to use again.
Now that you've seen how BOXY works, let's take a closer look at the thinking behind the design.
Every design decision was guided by three key ideas. Simpler folding. More efficient use of space. A cleaner travel experience.
To wrap up, BOXY is not simply about making a stroller smaller.
It's about making the entire travel experience simpler. Less time managing the stroller. More time enjoying the journey. Thank you for your time.
