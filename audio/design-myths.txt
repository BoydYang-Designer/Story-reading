One common idea I often question is that good design must always be something never before seen. In reality, originality only matters when it enhances function, evokes emotion, strengthens storytelling, or truly resonates with the user's experience. Being different for the sake of it isn't enough.

Users rely on familiar patterns to navigate products. If we change too much, we risk confusing or frustrating them. Good design isn't about constant invention, it's about making the best choices for the user.

Great designers know when to follow conventions and when to break them. They use creativity to enhance usability and experience, not just to stand out.
